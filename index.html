Hello, I'm Sima.

I obtained my MASc from Concordia University, Montréal, where I worked with Professors Lingyu Wang and Suryadipta Majumdar and contributed as a researcher to the NSERC/Ericsson IRC in SDN/NFV Security project. My research focused on optimal proactive attack detection and non-disruptive mitigation in cloud environments.

Research, ideas, and little observations about life.
