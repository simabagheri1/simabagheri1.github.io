I obtained my MASc from Concordia University, Montréal, where I worked in the NSERC/Ericsson Audit Ready Cloud (ARC) Research Group and contributed as a researcher to the NSERC/Ericsson IRC in SDN/NFV Security project. My research focused on optimal proactive attack detection and non-disruptive mitigation in cloud environments. Prior to that, I completed my education in in Software Engineering and Information Technology Engineering- Enterprise Architecture in Iran.
